Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy describes how LL Daily Automation (the "tool"), an internal publishing tool operated by Legal Loopholes Daily, handles data.

Who uses the tool

The tool is used only by the owner of Legal Loopholes Daily, to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

  • TikTok: an access token for the Legal Loopholes Daily TikTok account, and the basic account information TikTok provides with it (account ID, display name and avatar), used to publish videos to that account and to read the posting options TikTok requires.
  • YouTube: an authorization token for the Legal Loopholes Daily YouTube channel, used to upload videos and set their title, description, tags and privacy status. The tool uses YouTube API Services; the Google Privacy Policy also applies.
  • Instagram and Facebook: an access token for the Legal Loopholes Daily accounts, used to publish videos to them.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about any other person: no other users' profiles, videos, comments, messages or analytics.

Storage and sharing

Tokens are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, inside the automation software. They are never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and are used only for the purposes above.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from the app permissions settings of TikTok, Google/YouTube or Meta. Once revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the software. To request deletion of any data, email the contact below.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
